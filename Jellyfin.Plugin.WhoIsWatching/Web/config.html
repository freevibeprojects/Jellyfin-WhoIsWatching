Who's Watching

Brand text

Shown in a corner of the "Who's watching?" screen, like Netflix's logo. Leave the text empty to hide it. The rest of the screen follows Jellyfin's theme and Custom CSS (Dashboard, General, Branding); a line such as "Need help? Ask whoever set this up" goes in Jellyfin's Login Disclaimer there, shown under the profiles.

Back to the picker when idle

After this long with nobody touching the device and nothing playing, the picker comes back, so whoever comes next starts there. A profile not remembered on that device is signed out (others stay remembered). Takes effect the next time Jellyfin is opened.

Profile order

The order of the profiles in the picker, on the sign-in screen and from the avatar. Accounts hidden from the sign-in screen aren't listed there.

Who may add pictures

Untick someone to stop them adding pictures to the shared gallery; they can still pick from it. Administrators always may.

Profile pictures

The shared gallery people pick from under "Manage profiles". Pictures are added there: click your avatar, then Manage profiles, a profile, and the + tile. Removing a picture here doesn't change anyone's current profile picture.

Sign-in safety

The picker lists the same profiles as Jellyfin's sign-in page, and anyone at a device can try their passwords, also through Other account. Jellyfin doesn't limit failed attempts unless you ask it to: for each account, especially administrators, set the number of failed login attempts before lockout (Dashboard, Users, the user, Profile). Accounts hidden from the sign-in screen aren't listed, but can still be typed in. Jellyfin hides new accounts from the sign-in screen: to give someone a tile, untick "Hide this user from login screens" (Dashboard, Users, the user, Profile).
